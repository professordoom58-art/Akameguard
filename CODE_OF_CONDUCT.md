# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in AkameGuard a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Explain security concerns clearly and avoid sensationalism.
- Use harmless fixtures and redacted examples.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, private site data, or working
  attack payloads.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately adding malware, working web shells, or destructive test code.
- Misusing AkameGuard against systems without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's configured contact channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
confidentially where possible.
